Morning ritual

Four steps, about three minutes

Layer from thinnest to thickest and finish with sun protection. At night, skip step 4.

01

Cleanse

Massage the Facial Cleanser into damp skin and rinse. Salicylic acid clears pores without the tight feeling.

Morning and night
02

Drench

Press 3 to 4 drops of HA Serum into slightly damp skin for a plump, bright finish.

Morning and night
03

Calm

Smooth on Cica Cream. Centella and hyaluronic acid soothe and lock moisture in.

Morning and night
04

Protect

Finish with Sunscreen SPF 50+. Light, non-greasy, fragrance-free, UVA and UVB.
